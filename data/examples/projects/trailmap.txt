Project Name: TrailMap - Offline Hiking Planner

Summary:
A route planner that downloads trail maps for offline use and estimates hiking time from elevation data.

Architecture:
- FastAPI backend with PostGIS for spatial queries
- React PWA with service-worker map tile caching

Challenges:
- Elevation-aware time estimates: implemented Naismith's rule on PostGIS line segments.
